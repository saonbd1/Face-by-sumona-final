Contact Details and Opening Hours

Face by Sumona is at 121 Hazi Bari, Sher E Bangla Road, Middle Arichpur, Tongi 1710, Gazipur, Bangladesh. The salon is open seven days a week, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, so you can book a facial, haircut, threading or makeup appointment on any day of the week, including Friday and public holidays.

The quickest way to book is to call or WhatsApp +880 1933-211642. Tell us which service you want and when you would like to come in, and we will confirm the time. You can also send a message using the form above, or email facebysumona@gmail.com.

Getting here

The salon sits on Sher E Bangla Road in Middle Arichpur, easy to reach from Tongi Bazar, Tongi Station Road and the Tongi bridge area, and a short ride from Uttara and the rest of Gazipur. If you are arriving by rickshaw or CNG, ask for Sher E Bangla Road in Middle Arichpur and look for the Face by Sumona board.

Before you arrive

  • For facials, come with clean skin where possible and mention any sensitivity, allergy or product you are currently using.
  • For bridal and party makeup, book well ahead of your event so timings and any trial can be arranged.
  • If you are unsure which service suits you, call first — it is easier to advise before the appointment than to change course during it.
