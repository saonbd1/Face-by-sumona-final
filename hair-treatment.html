Which Hair Treatment Is Right for You?

Every woman's hair is unique, and daily routines like hair dryers, heat straighteners, hard water, dust, and humid weather can impact your hair health in different ways. Here is how to decide on the best treatment:

  • If your hair feels dull, heavy, or lifeless: Choose a Hair Spa Treatment. It clears away dead skin cells, excess sebum, and outdoor pollutants while revitalizing your scalp.
  • If your hair has split ends, breakage, or chemical damage: Choose a Hair Repair Treatment. This reconstructive formula infuses strengthening keratin and protein complexes into weakened hair fibers.
  • If you struggle with frizz, flyaways, or dry tangles: Choose a Deep Conditioning Treatment. It replenishes moisture, coats cuticles in protective hydration, and restores easy combing.
  • If you want herbal conditioning and natural richness: Choose a Henna Hair Treatment. It delivers chemical-free conditioning, enhances natural shine, and creates a fuller hair appearance.

Salon Hair Care & Aftercare Tips

To prolong the silky feel, shine, and health of your hair after visiting our Tongi parlor, follow these simple post-treatment habits:

  • Use a mild, sulfate-free shampoo: Gentle cleansers protect the moisture barrier and prevent stripping beneficial conditioning oils.
  • Avoid immediate heat styling: Give your hair at least 48 to 72 hours of rest from high-heat curling irons and straighteners.
  • Wash with lukewarm or cool water: Hot water opens the hair cuticle, leading to moisture loss and frizz; cool water seals the cuticle for extra shine.
  • Protect against outdoor dust and sun: In Tongi and Gazipur traffic and sunshine, tie hair loosely or use a light scarf when traveling.
  • Schedule regular salon upkeep: Visiting for a monthly hair spa or conditioning session ensures ongoing hair health and keeps split ends away.
