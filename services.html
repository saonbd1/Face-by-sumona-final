Beauty Services in Tongi — What We Offer

Face by Sumona offers skin, hair, makeup and grooming services in one place in Middle Arichpur, Tongi. Whether you are preparing for a wedding, refreshing your skin before an event, or simply keeping up with regular hair and brow maintenance, the salon is open seven days a week from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Treatments are chosen for your skin type and the result you want rather than applied as a fixed package. If you are unsure which facial or hair service suits you, call or WhatsApp +880 1933-211642 and we will advise you before you book.

Facials and Skin Treatments

Facials at Face by Sumona are grouped by what they are designed to do. A gold facial using the Active Plus kit refreshes and brightens the skin, which is why it is popular before weddings and parties. A HydraFacial on the 7-in-1 combo machine combines deep cleansing, exfoliation, suction and hydration in one session, and is a good choice if you want thorough pore cleansing without downtime.

If your skin is easily irritated, an herbal facial uses gentle herbal ingredients to cleanse and soothe, while a fruit facial uses fruit-based products for a milder, fresher treatment. A pearl facial and a whitening facial both work on the appearance of brightness and skin tone.

  • Mention any sensitivity, active acne, recent waxing or product you are using before the treatment begins — it changes what we recommend.
  • Arrive with clean skin where possible, and avoid strong sun exposure on the day of your appointment.

Haircuts, Hair Colour and Hair Care

Hair services run from a quick trim to a full colour change. Haircuts include bob, feather, layer, U-cut, step-cut and V-cut styles, so you can keep your length or take it shorter while keeping the shape suited to your face and hair type. Professional colouring is available to enhance your natural tone, and hair extensions are offered for added length or volume.

If you are deciding between styles, bring a reference photo or describe the look you want on the call — it is the fastest way for us to tell you what will work with your hair and how long the appointment will take.

Bridal and Party Makeup

Bridal makeover is one of the salon's main services. Makeup is tailored to your outfit, your skin tone and the lighting of your event, and on-home service is available so the look can be created where you are getting ready. Party and event makeup, casual day makeup, and professional photo-shoot makeup are also offered.

For bridal bookings, contact us as early as you can with the date and your schedule for the day. Trial discussions and timings are easier to arrange before wedding season fills up.
