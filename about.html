Training, Experience and Approach

Sumona has worked as a beautician since 2004, and Face by Sumona has become a familiar name for beauty and salon services among women in Tongi and nearby Uttara. Over that time the work has stayed deliberately broad: facials and skincare, hair cutting and colouring, threading and waxing, nail services, and bridal makeup for weddings and events.

The approach is personal. Rather than applying the same treatment and the same look to everyone, services are chosen for your skin type, your features and the occasion you are preparing for — and, for bridal work, for your outfit and the lighting of the day itself. Products are chosen for safe, lasting results, and the salon environment is kept clean and comfortable.

If you are booking for the first time, mention what you are hoping to achieve. It is far easier to advise you on the right facial, cut or makeup style before the appointment than to change course during it.
