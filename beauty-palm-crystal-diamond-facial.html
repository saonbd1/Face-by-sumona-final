What the Beauty Palm Crystal Diamond Facial Does

The Beauty Palm Crystal Diamond Facial is an intensive, revitalising treatment tailored for women looking to counter the daily toll of weather, environmental pollution, and stress on their skin. Living and travelling around Tongi, Gazipur, and Dhaka exposes the face to persistent dust, intense sunlight, and atmospheric grime that settle into pores and cause a dull, tired appearance.

This multi-step kit works methodically to lift impurities, shed dull outer cells, and saturate the skin with essential moisture and minerals. Certified beautician Sumona personally guides each application, ensuring the pressure, duration, and sequence cater precisely to your skin's texture.

  • Deep Pore Purification: The combination of smoothing toner and fine whitening scrub lifts dirt, dissolves stubborn sebum plugs, and sweeps away dead cells without irritating delicate tissue.
  • Active Hydration & Soothing: The moisturising cream replenishes the skin barrier, calming areas of tightness, roughness, or dryness caused by weather and pollution.
  • Mineral Detoxification: The cooling mud mask draws out deeper impurities, tightens enlarged pores, and helps balance excess shine.
  • Luminous Diamond Finish: The final whitening cream massage stimulates blood circulation, restores evenness, and imparts a healthy, glowing diamond sheen.

Before and After Your Diamond Facial

To achieve the best possible glow and longevity from your treatment, a little preparation and mindful post-treatment care will ensure your skin stays radiant and calm.

Before your session, inform Sumona if your skin is feeling extra sensitive, if you have active sunburn, or if you recently underwent bleaching, threading, or waxing. For clients with sensitive skin or allergies, a quick patch test is always recommended prior to applying the full kit.

  • Before Appointment: Skip harsh physical scrubs, chemical peels, or strong exfoliants for 24 to 48 hours beforehand. Arrive with bare skin whenever possible.
  • Immediate Aftercare: Allow the nourishing creams and serums to settle into your skin. Avoid washing your face with harsh soaps for at least 6 to 8 hours after the facial.
  • Sun Protection: Apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen if you are stepping outdoors into the daytime sun or dust.
  • Post-Event Timing: If you are attending a wedding or special party, booking your Diamond Facial 1 to 2 days before the event allows the skin optimal time to settle into its peak glow.
