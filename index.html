A Beauty Salon for Women in Tongi Since 2004

Face by Sumona is a beauty salon for women in Middle Arichpur, Tongi, on Sher E Bangla Road. Certified beautician Sumona has been caring for clients in Tongi, Gazipur and nearby Uttara since 2004, and the salon is open seven days a week from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

The work covers skin, hair and makeup together rather than one narrow speciality. That means you can book a facial to refresh dull or tired skin, a haircut or colour change, eyebrow threading and waxing, a manicure, or a full bridal makeover — and have them planned around the same occasion.

Appointments are personal rather than rushed. Every treatment is chosen for your skin type and the result you want, using professional products and a clean, comfortable salon environment.

  • Skin: gold (Active Plus), HydraFacial 7-in-1, herbal, pearl, whitening and fruit facials, plus face treatments and clean-up.
  • Hair: haircuts and styling — bob, feather, layer, U, step and V cuts — colouring, and hair extensions.
  • Makeup: bridal makeover, party and event makeup, casual day makeup and professional photo-shoot makeup. On-home service is available.
  • Hands & grooming: nail art, manicure, eyebrow threading, facial threading and waxing.
