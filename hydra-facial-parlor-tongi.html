How the HydraFacial 7-in-1 Treatment Works

HydraFacial is a non-invasive skincare treatment that combines several steps into one seamless session. Using advanced suction, hydration and infusion techniques, it deeply cleanses pores, removes impurities, and delivers essential nutrients directly to the skin. Unlike a traditional facial, the machine works with precision and consistency, which is why results are often visible after just one session.

At Face by Sumona in Tongi, the treatment runs on the HydraFacial 7-in-1 Combo Machine. Each of the steps targets a different job, so the whole surface of the face is cleansed and then rehydrated in the same appointment.

  • Ultrasound therapy: helps loosen congestion beneath the surface of the skin.
  • Deep cleansing and exfoliation: removes dead skin cells and unclogs pores.
  • Hydration therapy: infuses hydrating serums to leave skin plump and comfortable.
  • Oxygen infusion: a final step that helps the skin look refreshed rather than tight or dry.

What to Expect and How to Prepare

HydraFacial is a comfortable, non-invasive treatment; most clients describe it as a cool pressure moving across the skin rather than a painful sensation. There is generally no downtime, so many people are able to return to their day afterwards.

Before your appointment, mention any sensitivity, recent treatments or products you are using. People with sensitive or dry skin should patch test first and follow the recommended frequency to help reduce the risk of irritation. If your main concern is dark spots, understand that the treatment is intended to help reduce their appearance and brighten the complexion — results vary and daily skincare still matters.

HydraFacial — Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Hydra 7-in-1 face treatment suitable for all skin types?

The Hydra machine works differently for different skin types. People with sensitive or dry skin should patch test first and follow the recommended frequency to help reduce the risk of irritation.

Does the machine remove dark spots permanently?

The Hydra kit is intended to help reduce the appearance of dark spots and brighten the look of the complexion. Results may vary, and daily care of your skin is necessary.

Do I need to have this facial often?

No, you do not have to. Many clients book a HydraFacial before an event or occasionally as part of their routine. We will recommend a frequency for your skin type.

How long does it take to see results?

The time required to see visible changes varies by person and depends on consistent use, skin type and other skincare habits. But you will notice the change yourself.
